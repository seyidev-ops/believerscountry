Home / Spiritual Intent

Scripture, read in the light of Christ.

Interpreting Scripture in the light of the Son, the new creation and His finished work.

Christ the foundation. Scripture the witness.

Spiritual Intent is a developing literary and teaching identity that examines the whole counsel of Scripture in the light of Christ Jesus. Its approach is attentive to context, fulfillment, the finished work of Christ, and the life of the believer as a new creation.

It is connected to CMI’s doctrinal framework, but its written works make room for extended argument, confirmation passages and practical application — beyond a short caption or isolated quotation.

The growing collections

Money Under God gathers studies about provision, receiving, debt, investment and enjoyment. The Business of the Believer develops practical scriptural wisdom about work and business through the created order.
