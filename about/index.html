The country we call home.

Believers Country welcomes you into the shared life and ministry of Country Ministries International.

A people belonging to the Father. A life received in the Son. A country expressed through His Spirit.

Ephesians 2:18–22 places our belonging in the work of the Son and the fellowship of the Spirit: we have access to the Father and are joined as fellow citizens and members of His household. John 14:16–23 shows how that shared life is personally received and lived.

We begin with the Son.

Our teaching, community life and ministry expressions are interpreted through Christ Jesus, His person and His finished work. That is the centre from which we approach Scripture, the new creation, the believer’s identity and our shared practice.

“Country” communicates belonging, shared life, citizenship, culture and ordered community under Christ. It is not a claim to political sovereignty, nor a literal translation of the Greek term basileia.

The four streams

Believers Country is the communal expression; School of Scriptures provides teaching and formation; Commonwealth addresses practical stewardship and care; Country Vanguard gives expression to mission and outreach.
