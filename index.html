A country of life · Concept 03

More than a gathering.
A shared life.

Believers Country is the welcoming communal expression of CMI. Here, our understanding of Scripture, our fellowship and our service begin with Christ Jesus: who He is, what He has accomplished and the life we have received in Him.

We are learning to express that life together — in the Word, in prayer, in practical care and beyond our immediate community.
