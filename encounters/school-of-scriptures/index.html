School of Scriptures

Study the Scriptures in the light of Christ.

The School of Scriptures encounter is a regular time of patient, Christ-centred study. Scripture is considered with its context, confirmation passages and implications for the life of the believer.

This weekly gathering belongs to the wider School of Scriptures ministry.

What to expect

Come with an open heart, your Scriptures and a willingness to participate in a Christ-centred gathering. Any special theme, additional study notes or confirmed venue information will appear here when issued by the ministry.
