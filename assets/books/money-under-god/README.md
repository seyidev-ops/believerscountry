# Money Under God artwork origin
Four panels have been cropped without redrawing from `Money Under God four-book collection(2).png` (later Spiritual Intent project graphic, 11 October 2026). The exact source is copied into this folder as `source-money-under-god-2026-10-11.png`. Both the composite and each cover remain otherwise untouched.

The second book correctly reads **How to Receive From God — Enjoying the life to come, here and now**; the older 40-patterns subtitle is not carried forward. Other titles and subtitles are original to the identified source. These images are source-bounded raster crops of a combined image, rather than available standalone cover masters. Use higher-resolution originals only when the exact approved artwork is supplied.
