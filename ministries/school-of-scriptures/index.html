School of Scriptures

The ministry’s teaching, study, formation and publishing expression, with Christ as interpretive centre.

About this streamHow it worksTake part

The School exists because a movement that cannot reproduce its reasoning will eventually reproduce only its slogans. Its mandate is to teach how conclusions are reached, not merely which conclusions to repeat.

Luke 24, Acts 17:11 and 2 Timothy 2:15 set the culture: Scripture opened around Christ, claims examined, and workers trained to handle the word accurately. The Wednesday study is one regular gathering of this ministry; its wider work includes teaching, study and publishing.

How it works.

  1. ChristologyThe person and finished work of the Son.
  2. New creation identityWhat is true of the one who is in Christ.
  3. The Holy SpiritHis person, mission, gifts, fruit and the testing of what we hear.
  4. Languages and sacred timeWord studies, the Hebrew calendar and their responsible use.
